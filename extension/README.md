# 🚀 ApplyPilot AI - Chrome Extension (Manifest V3)

Automate LinkedIn Easy Apply, instant AI-crafted recruiter outreach notes, and Google Forms directly in Google Chrome / Brave / Edge — with zero Python or ChromeDriver setup required.

---

## ⚡ 1-Minute Quick Start

### Step 1: Open Chrome Extensions
1. Open Google Chrome (or Brave / Edge / Arc).
2. Navigate to: `chrome://extensions` (or `brave://extensions`).
3. In the top right corner, toggle **Developer mode** to **ON**.

---

### Step 2: Load the Extension
1. Click the **"Load unpacked"** button in the top left.
2. Select the **`extension`** folder from this project directory:
   ```
   /Users/pulkit/Downloads/AAT/extension
   ```
3. ApplyPilot AI is now installed and ready! Pin it to your Chrome toolbar for easy access.

---

## 🎯 How to Use

### 1. Configure Your Profile & AI Key
- Click the **ApplyPilot AI** icon in your toolbar ➔ Click **Settings (⚙️)**.
- Confirm your candidate details (Name, Experience, Headline, Summary).
- Your Groq API key is pre-configured and ready for ultra-fast AI inference.

### 2. Auto-Apply on LinkedIn
1. Open any LinkedIn Job Search (e.g. [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?f_AL=true)).
2. Look at the bottom right of your screen: the **ApplyPilot AI Floating HUD** will appear.
3. Click **▶ Start**.
4. The extension will automatically:
   - Match job requirements against your experience cutoff.
   - Fill Easy Apply questions and forms using Groq AI.
   - Connect with the hiring manager/recruiter with a personalized note (<= 300 chars).
   - Protect your paid InMail credits by using free direct messages.

### 3. Auto-Fill Google Forms
1. Open any hiring Google Form (`forms.gle/...`).
2. The ApplyPilot HUD will appear automatically.
3. Click **▶ Start** to let Groq AI read the questions and fill your answers instantly.

### 4. Export Your Job Tracker CSV
- Click the extension icon in the toolbar ➔ Click **📥 Export CSV** to download your full log of applied jobs and recruiter outreach notes!
